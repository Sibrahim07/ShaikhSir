The DevOps Philosophy

Lesson

DevOps is a compound of Development and Operations. It is a set of cultural philosophies, practices, and automated tools that increases an organization’s ability to deliver applications and services at high velocity.

The Traditional Wall of Confusion

Historically, software developers wrote code and threw it over the "wall" to IT operations, who were measured solely on system stability. This created opposing incentives: developers wanted rapid change, while operations resisted change to prevent outages.

DevOps dismantles this divide by creating cross-functional teams with shared accountability for the entire application lifecycle — from design and coding to deployment and production monitoring.
